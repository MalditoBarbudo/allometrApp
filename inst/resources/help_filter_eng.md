### Allometry Selection

The available allometries can be filtered by their different characteristics:  

- By dependent variable  
- By independent variable(s)  
- By allometry level (plant or organ)  
- By spatial level of allometry (national, region, province, district)  
- By functional group level (genus, species group, species)  
- By specific functional group (specific species or genus)  
- By volume calculation method (defined in the inventories)  
- For *Quercus suber*, by the value of the special parameter (defined in the inventories)

### Downloading the Allometry Table

The allometry table, either the entire table or the selected ones, can be downloaded by clicking on the button corresponding to the desired format (`csv` or `xlsx`).


### Allometry Calculation

The calculation tab allows you to select a data file (csv or xlsx) and the allometries to be calculated, as well as map the file variables to the allometry variables. The calculation is performed instantly when everything is selected, and the result can be downloaded by clicking the corresponding button.
